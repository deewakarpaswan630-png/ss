Project Overview

A Modern Landmark
in Gurugram

Indiabulls Heights Sector 104 Gurgaon is a premium high-rise residential project offering luxury 2 BHK + Utility and 3 BHK apartments at ₹15,000/sq.ft. Spread across 4 towers with 38 floors, the development is designed for modern urban living with spacious layouts, fully modular kitchens, wide balconies, and top-tier interiors.

Situated on a 6-acre plot with a low-density layout, the project ensures privacy and peace, surrounded by lush greenery. With direct access to Dwarka Expressway and NH-48, it is conveniently close to Delhi, IGI Airport, Diplomatic Enclave, and Yashobhoomi Convention Centre.
